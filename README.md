# POLARIS TWINS

### Digital Twin Platform for Remote Management of Antarctic Research Stations

POLARIS TWINS is a Digital Twin-based platform designed to support the remote monitoring, simulation, and intelligent management of Antarctic research stations.

The platform brings together environmental conditions, energy systems, infrastructure, logistics, and operational risks into a unified digital environment.

Its goal is to move from simple monitoring to **predictive decision support** by allowing operators to understand current conditions, simulate possible failures, and assess their operational impact.

---

## 🌐 Overview

Antarctic research stations operate in extremely harsh and isolated environments where physical access is difficult and communication bandwidth can be limited.

A failure in one system can quickly affect other critical operations.
