## 🌐 Overview

**POLARIS TWINS** is a Digital Twin-based platform designed to enable efficient and intelligent remote management of India's Antarctic research stations, **Maitri and Bharati**.

Antarctic stations operate in extreme environmental conditions and face challenges such as limited connectivity, difficult physical access, resource constraints, equipment failures, and unpredictable weather.

POLARIS creates a connected digital representation of station operations by integrating:

- 🌡️ Environmental conditions
- ⚡ Energy and power systems
- 🏗️ Infrastructure
- 🚢 Logistics and resources
- ⚠️ Operational risks
- 📊 Predictive analytics
- 🔄 What-if simulation

The goal is to move from **reactive monitoring to predictive decision support**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Remote management of Antarctic research stations is challenging because of:

- Extreme weather conditions
- Geographical isolation
- Limited communication bandwidth
- Dependence on fuel and critical resources
- Difficulty in physical maintenance and intervention
- Interdependency between power, infrastructure, weather and logistics

A failure in one system can create a cascading impact on other station operations.

POLARIS addresses this challenge by providing a unified Digital Twin platform that allows operators to monitor conditions, simulate scenarios and identify potential operational risks.

## 💡 Our Solution

POLARIS TWINS uses a **coupled Digital Twin architecture** connecting the major operational components of Antarctic stations.

### 1. Coupled Digital Twin

Connects station-level:

- Energy
- Infrastructure
- Logistics
- Environmental conditions

into a unified operational view.

### 2. Multi-Source Data Integration

The platform is designed to combine information from different sources such as:

- Weather data
- Power plant telemetry
- Generator status
- Fuel levels
- Infrastructure parameters
- Resource and logistics information

### 3. Predictive Operational Analytics

The system can analyse current conditions and estimate their operational impact.
